Facilities

    Sports and Recreation Facilities
  • Indoor Sports Facility: Spans over 1,198 square meters.
  • Outdoor Ground: Large open sports ground covering 5,000 square meters.
  • Rooftop Play Area & Turf: Located on the 8th floor for recreation and play.
    Academic and Advanced Labs
  • Specialized Labs: Includes an AI Lab, Robotics Lab, STEM Lab, and Language Lab.
  • Digital Library: Equipped with modern digital learning resources.
  • Virtual Classrooms: Technology-enabled spaces for interactive learning.
  • Home Economics Lab: Practical learning space for students.
  • Art Studio: Dedicated creative space for arts and craft.
    Campus and Support Amenities
  • Auditorium: Spans over 250 square meters for events and gatherings.
  • Canteen / Dining: Facilities to support its tiffin-less/nutritional daily program.
  • Counselling Room: Dedicated space for student guidance and support.
  • Personal Lockers: Provided to enable bagless learning by replacing heavy school bags.
