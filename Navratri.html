Navratri - The Festival of Divine Energy

What is Navratri?

Navratri is a major Hindu festival celebrated over nine nights and ten days. It honors the nine forms of Goddess Durga and symbolizes the victory of good over evil.

Why is it Celebrated?

Navratri commemorates the battle between Goddess Durga and the demon Mahishasura. It celebrates the triumph of divine feminine power and spiritual strength.

How is Navratri Celebrated?

  • Each day is dedicated to a different form of Goddess Durga
  • Devotees observe fasting and perform rituals
  • Colorful dances like Garba and Dandiya Raas are performed
  • Temples and homes are decorated with lights and flowers
  • Special prayers and offerings are made

Types of Navratri

  • Sharad Navratri: Celebrated in autumn (most popular)
  • Chaitra Navratri: Celebrated in spring
  • Magha and Ashadha Navratri: Lesser-known seasonal observances

Fun Fact

Each day of Navratri is associated with a specific color, and devotees often wear clothes matching that day's color to honor the goddess.
