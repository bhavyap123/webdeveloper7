Ganesh Chaturthi - The Festival of Lord Ganesha

What is Ganesh Chaturthi?

Ganesh Chaturthi, also known as Vinayaka Chaturthi, is a Hindu festival that celebrates the birth of Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the god of wisdom and prosperity.

Why is it Celebrated?

The festival marks the day Goddess Parvati created Lord Ganesha from sandalwood paste. It is believed that worshipping Ganesha brings good fortune and removes obstacles from one’s life.

How is Ganesh Chaturthi Celebrated?

  • Clay idols of Lord Ganesha are installed in homes and public pandals
  • Daily prayers, aartis, and offerings of sweets like modak are made
  • Cultural events and music performances are held
  • On the final day, idols are immersed in water (Visarjan) with chants of “Ganpati Bappa Morya!”

Duration of the Festival

The festival usually lasts for 10 days, starting on the fourth day of the Hindu month of Bhadrapada and ending on Anant Chaturdashi.

Fun Fact

Ganesh Chaturthi was popularized as a public celebration by freedom fighter Lokmanya Tilak in 1893 to unite people during British rule.
