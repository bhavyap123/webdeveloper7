FAQ

Plastic and air pollution are two of the most pressing environmental issues facing the world today. Plastic pollution is a cross-border problem that requires international cooperation to address, while air pollution is responsible for about 7 million premature deaths globally each year. As such, it is important to ask questions about the causes and consequences of these types of pollution, as well as potential solutions. For example, regarding plastic pollution, can recycling alone end the crisis? How can the world tackle plastic pollution? What is the world doing about it? When it comes to air pollution, what are the health effects of particle pollution? How can individuals reduce their exposure? What are the sources of air pollution? These are just a few of the many questions that can be asked about pollution, and finding the answers is crucial for protecting the health of people and the planet.
