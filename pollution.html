pollution

Predominantly caused by vehicle emissions, industrial activity, burning of fossil fuels, and construction dust. Pune faces challenges from rising vehicle numbers and industrial growth contributing to smog and particulate matter. Water Pollution: Contamination of rivers, lakes, and groundwater due to untreated sewage, industrial discharges, and chemical runoff. The Mula-Mutha river in Pune has been identified as highly polluted. Soil Pollution: Resulting from the disposal of hazardous wastes, use of pesticides and fertilizers, and dumping of non-biodegradable garbage. Noise Pollution: Increasing due to traffic, construction, and urbanization which affects human health, including hearing loss and stress. Light Pollution: Excessive artificial light in urban areas disrupting ecosystems and affecting human sleep patterns. Causes of Pollution in Pune and Urban India Rapid urbanization leading to increased vehicles and industrialization without commensurate pollution control measures. Poor waste management and inadequate sewage treatment
