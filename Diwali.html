Diwali - The Festival of Lights

What is Diwali?

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is one of the most important festivals in India. It celebrates the victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

How is it Celebrated?

  • Lighting oil lamps (diyas) and candles
  • Decorating homes with rangoli
  • Worshipping Goddess Lakshmi
  • Exchanging gifts and sweets
  • Fireworks and family gatherings

Significance

Diwali marks the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya after defeating Ravana. It is also associated with the worship of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth.

Fun Fact

Diwali is celebrated not only by Hindus, but also by Jains, Sikhs, and some Buddhists, each with their own traditions.
