Major Types of Pollution Air Pollution: Contaminates the atmosphere with harmful gases (e.g., carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides), particulate matter, and chemicals from vehicles, factories, and burning fossil fuels. Air pollution causes respiratory diseases, cardiovascular problems, smog, acid rain, and contributes to climate change Water Pollution: Involves the contamination of rivers, lakes, oceans, and groundwater by industrial discharges, sewage, agricultural runoff, oil spills, and plastic waste. This leads to illness, loss of aquatic life, and unsafe drinking water Soil Pollution: Results from excessive use of pesticides, improper waste dumping, industrial chemicals, and heavy metals, reducing soil fertility and contaminating the food chain Noise Pollution: Excessive and harmful sounds from traffic, industrial activities, and urbanization can cause hearing loss, stress, and disturb wildlife Light Pollution: Artificial light disrupting natural darkness, affecting ecosystems, human sleep patterns, and wasting energy Thermal Pollution: Sudden changes in water temperature due to industrial discharge harming aquatic ecosystems Radioactive Pollution: Release of radioactive substances from nuclear accidents or improper waste disposal causing long-term environmental and health hazards Plastic Pollution: Accumulation of plastic waste in the environment that poses serious threats to wildlife and human health.
