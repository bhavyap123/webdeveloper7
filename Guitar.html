Guitar

The guitar is one of the most popular and versatile musical instruments in the world, admired for its ability to produce rich harmonies, melodies, and rhythms across countless genres. It belongs to the chordophone family and is typically played by strumming or plucking strings stretched over a hollow or solid body.
