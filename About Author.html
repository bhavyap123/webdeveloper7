About Author

  • Gaur Gopal Das

    Gaur Gopal Das is a prominent Indian ISKCON monk, lifestyle coach, motivational speaker, and bestselling author who translates ancient spiritual wisdom into practical advice for contemporary life. A former electrical engineer at Hewlett-Packard, he altered his life trajectory in 1996 by joining the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) ashram in Mumbai. Over the past three decades, his humorous, anecdotal, and highly relatable teaching style has earned him a global digital following of over 17 million people.


  • A.P.J Abdul Kalam

    Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (15 October 1931 – 27 July 2015) was a world-renowned aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Widely revered as the "Missile Man of India" and the "People's President," he rose from an impoverished background to become one of the country's most iconic scientific and political leaders.


  • Morgan Housel

    In his modern personal finance classic, The Psychology of Money: Timeless Lessons on Wealth, Greed, and Happiness, investor and journalist Morgan Housel argues that doing well with money is not about how smart you are, but entirely about how you behave. Rather than treating finance as a rigid mathematical science, Housel teaches that financial success is a soft skill governed by human psychology, ego, and emotion.


  • Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni

    Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni is an award-winning Indian-born American author, poet, and professor known for her powerful storytelling centered on the immigrant experience, women's empowerment, and mythological retellings.


  • Ruskin Bond

    Ruskin Bond is a legendary Indian author of British descent who has spent over seven decades shaping Indian English literature. Known as the pioneer of children's literature in India, his deeply humanistic writing features simple language, vivid depictions of nature, and memorable settings in the Himalayan foothills. Over his prolific career, Bond has authored more than 500 short stories, essays, and novels, alongside over 50 children's books.
