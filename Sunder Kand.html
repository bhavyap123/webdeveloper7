Sunder Kand – The Glorious Journey of Hanuman

Introduction

Sunder Kand is the fifth chapter of the epic Ramayana. It focuses on Hanuman’s heroic journey to Lanka in search of Sita. This section is celebrated for its spiritual depth, courage, and devotion.

Setting: From Kishkindha to Lanka

Hanuman sets out from the Vanara kingdom of Kishkindha, crosses the ocean, and enters Lanka—the kingdom of Ravana. His mission is to find Sita and deliver Rama’s message of hope.

Key Events

  • Hanuman leaps across the ocean to reach Lanka
  • Encounters with Surasa, Simhika, and Lankini
  • Discovery of Sita in Ashoka Vatika
  • Hanuman delivers Rama’s message to Sita
  • Hanuman’s battle with Ravana’s forces
  • Burning of Lanka and return to Rama

Themes

  • Devotion and Faith
  • Courage and Strength
  • Hope and Determination
  • Victory of Good over Evil

Emotional Impact

Sunder Kand is deeply inspiring. Hanuman’s unwavering devotion to Rama and his fearless actions uplift the spirit and instill faith. It’s often recited for strength, protection, and blessings.

Legacy

This Kand is one of the most popular and widely recited sections of the Ramayana. It is considered highly auspicious and is often read during times of difficulty or spiritual practice.
