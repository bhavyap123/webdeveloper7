Holi - The Festival of Colors

What is Holi?

Holi is a vibrant Hindu festival celebrated across India and many parts of the world. It marks the arrival of spring and the triumph of good over evil.

Why is Holi Celebrated?

Holi commemorates the legend of Prahlada and Holika, symbolizing the victory of devotion and righteousness. It also celebrates the playful love of Radha and Krishna.

How is Holi Celebrated?

  • People throw colored powders (gulal) and water at each other
  • Bonfires are lit the night before (Holika Dahan)
  • Families gather, share sweets, and dance to music
  • Traditional foods like gujiya and thandai are enjoyed

Fun Facts

Holi is known for breaking social barriers—everyone joins in the fun regardless of age, status, or background. The phrase “Holi Hai!” echoes through the streets during the celebration.
