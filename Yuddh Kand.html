Yuddh Kand – The War of Dharma

Introduction

Yuddh Kand, also known as Lanka Kand, is the sixth chapter of the epic Ramayana. It narrates the great war between Lord Rama and Ravana, culminating in the triumph of good over evil and the reunion of Rama and Sita.

Setting: The Battlefield of Lanka

The events unfold in Lanka, the golden city ruled by Ravana. Rama, with the help of the Vanara army led by Hanuman and Sugriva, prepares for battle to rescue Sita and restore dharma.

Key Events

  • Construction of the bridge to Lanka (Rama Setu)
  • Arrival of Rama’s army in Lanka
  • Fierce battles between Vanaras and Rakshasas
  • Slaying of Ravana’s powerful allies like Kumbhakarna and Indrajit
  • Rama’s final battle with Ravana
  • Victory of Rama and rescue of Sita
  • Agni Pariksha (Sita’s trial by fire)
  • Return to Ayodhya in the Pushpaka Vimana

Themes

  • Triumph of Good over Evil
  • Honor, Courage, and Sacrifice
  • Justice and Dharma
  • Devotion and Loyalty

Emotional Impact

Yuddh Kand is intense and dramatic, filled with moments of valor, grief, and divine intervention. Rama’s unwavering commitment to righteousness and Sita’s dignity leave a lasting impression.

Legacy

This Kand is often recited during Dussehra and Diwali celebrations. It symbolizes the eternal victory of truth and virtue, and remains one of the most revered sections of the Ramayana.
