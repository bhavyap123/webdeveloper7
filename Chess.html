Chess

Chess is one of the world’s oldest and most strategic board games, celebrated for its blend of intellect, creativity, and competition. Originating in northern India around the 6th century as chaturanga, it spread through Persia and the Islamic world before reaching Europe, where it evolved into the modern form we know today. Played on an 8×8 checkered board with 64 squares, chess involves two players controlling 16 pieces each — pawns, rooks, knights, bishops, a queen, and a king — with the ultimate goal of checkmating the opponent’s king.Chess is a timeless game of strategy and intellect, played and admired across cultures for centuries. It originated in India around the 6th century as chaturanga, symbolizing the four divisions of the military — infantry, cavalry, elephants, and chariots — which later evolved into pawns, knights, bishops, and rooks. From India, it spread to Persia as shatranj, then through the Islamic world into Europe, where the modern rules were established in the 15th century.
